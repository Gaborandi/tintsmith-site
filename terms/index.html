Terms

Tintsmith offers free content with optional permanent in-app purchases through the App Store. Apple’s applicable terms of sale govern transactions and refund requests.

Purchases

Complete unlocks the remaining Journey acts, painting studies, Contracts, Masters palettes, and Atelier pigments described on the purchase screen. Patron is a separate optional support purchase that adds two atmosphere themes. Storefront prices are supplied by Apple.

Use Restore Purchases to check previously purchased access. Purchase availability, Family Sharing eligibility, and refunds are governed by the product configuration and Apple’s applicable terms. Purchases and saved game progress are separate.

The game

Free play may show occasional ads between Journey plates and optional rewarded ads for 30 minutes of active play without ads. Tintsmith has no banners, subscriptions, energy system, paid hints, or paid retries. Complete removes all ads and ad offers. Its authored pigment model and painting activities are a game simulation, not a promise of physical paint accuracy or a conservation tool.

Your progress

Core play is local. Purchases, restore, and iCloud synchronization depend on Apple’s services. Keep manual exports of supported progress you want to retain. Unfinished Contracts and settings are device-local and excluded from progress exports. Do not assume synchronization keeps a separate backup history.
