A small colour-mixing lesson

One red. Five white.
Cherry Blossom pink.

The pigment names are only half the puzzle. The amounts matter too. Here’s one recipe you can follow in Tintsmith.

1 part vermilion
+ 5 parts lead white

Six pigment units altogether. No Prussian blue in this mixture.

This example is from Journey → The Floating World → plate 8, which requires Tintsmith Complete.

Choose the amounts

  1. Start with an empty bowl on the Cherry Blossom plate.
  2. Tap vermilion once and lead white five times. Check the small amount badges: 1 and 5.
  3. Tap MIX, then compare “yours” with the “plate” target.

Compare the result

The recorded attempt below earned three stars. The two halves of the result swatch let you compare the mixture with the target.

What does 1:5 mean?

For every one part of vermilion, this recipe uses five parts of lead white. It does not mean five parts total. Counting the six units makes the shade easier to repeat.

Tintsmith uses simulated pigments and an authored mixing model. This is a recipe for the game, not an instruction for mixing physical paints.
