The painter’s puzzle

Coming to iPhone and iPad · not yet released

Painting studies arrive with their color gone. Mix each color in your bowl and bring the painting back, one region at a time.

Begin with an illustrated study, then explore a detailed painted interpretation. Choose whole, named forms to paint; delicate focal details stay finished, and fabric decoration returns with its surrounding cloth. The painted collection uses AI-assisted artwork adapted for Tintsmith’s color puzzles.

Experiment with eighteen simulated pigments inspired by historical palettes. An authored spectral model gives each mixture consistent rules to discover; it does not reproduce measured physical paints.

A studio to explore

Follow 240 Journey plates, visit the free daily Salon, earn fifty Masters palettes, restore paintings in Commissions, experiment in the Atelier, and keep your work in the Library.

begin freely

Start with all 40 Cave plates and the first chapter of the Tomb, the Salon, The Wave restoration and Contract, two Masters palettes, and four Atelier pigments. The studio opens room by room as you paint. Tintsmith Complete permanently opens the remaining content. Optional Patron support adds two atmosphere themes.

the covenant

No banners or ads during puzzles. Free play includes occasional ads between Journey plates and an optional ad for 30 minutes of active play without ads. Complete removes all ads. No subscriptions, energy, paid hints, or paid retries. Core play works offline. Purchases, restore, and iCloud synchronization need Apple’s services. Launch language: English.

Your work

Keep your progress on your device and export a manual copy of supported progress and collections. Unfinished Contracts and settings stay on their original device and are excluded from progress exports. Settings shows iCloud sync availability.
