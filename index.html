Tintsmith

The painter’s puzzle

Available now on iPhone and iPad

Painting studies arrive with their color gone. Mix each color in your bowl and bring the painting back, one region at a time.

Begin with three short painting lessons, then explore twelve detailed painted interpretations. Mix the colours and restore numbered areas. The painted collection uses AI-assisted artwork adapted for Tintsmith’s color puzzles.

Experiment with twenty-one simulated pigments inspired by historical palettes. An authored spectral model gives each mixture consistent rules to discover; it does not reproduce measured physical paints.

A studio to explore

Follow 240 Journey plates, visit the free daily Salon, earn fifty Masters palettes, restore paintings in Commissions, experiment in the Atelier, and keep your work in the Library.

begin freely

Start with all 40 Cave plates and a sampler of the next act, three painting lessons, The Lantern Tide painting, the daily Salon and selected studio activities. The studio opens room by room as you paint. Tintsmith Complete permanently opens the remaining content. Optional Patron support adds two atmosphere themes.

the covenant

No banners or ads during puzzles. Free play includes occasional ads between Journey plates and an optional ad for 30 minutes of active play without ads. Complete removes all ads. No subscriptions, energy, paid hints, or paid retries. Core play works offline. Purchases, restore, and iCloud synchronization need Apple’s services. Launch language: English.

Your work

Keep your progress on your device and export a manual copy of supported progress and collections. Unfinished Contracts and settings stay on their original device and are excluded from progress exports. Settings shows iCloud sync availability.

Try a colour-mixing recipe

Mix Cherry Blossom pink: one vermilion, five lead white. See the amounts and the recorded result in a short illustrated guide.
